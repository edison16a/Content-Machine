# Responsible use

Content Machine re-cuts long videos into short ones. That is only fair when you have the right to do it.

## Only use footage you may use

- Your own videos.
- Footage from an official clipping program, within that program's rules.
- A creator's videos when they have agreed, and only for the kind of use they agreed to.

**Sequential mode re-posts a whole video in parts.** It needs permission that clearly covers full-length or multi-part use. Many clipping programs allow clips only; if a `source/brief.txt` says "clips only", the playbook will not use Sequential.

If you cannot say where the footage came from, do not use it. The playbook asks once at the start and stops if the answer does not cover the use.

## What the tool will not do

- **No downloading.** The repo contains no video downloader and never will. You supply your own files and transcripts.
- **No audio changes.** The original audio is kept as it is. No music, trending sounds or loudness changes are added.
- **No made-up content.** Titles must describe what really happens, and any tease must pay off in the video.
- **No logins.** Claude never asks for or types passwords or two-factor codes. Posting happens through browser sessions you are already logged in to.

## Credit and platform rules

Every video carries a credit line with the source platform's logo and the channel name, and every caption includes a credit line unless a campaign brief says otherwise. Follow each platform's community guidelines, branded content rules and disclosure requirements, and any campaign brief you are working under.
